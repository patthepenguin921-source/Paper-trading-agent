# Research library

## Built-in training material (maintained by the owner)
- `curriculum/00-start-here.md` … `08-research-workflow.md`: the core training course.
  It's distilled into `memory/knowledge.md` during study runs.
- `stocks/*.md`: dossiers on every core-universe stock and ETF, plus a checklist for
  researching anything else:
  - `tech-semis-software.md`
  - `internet-media-telecom-autos.md`
  - `financials-healthcare.md`
  - `consumer-industrials.md`
  - `energy-materials-utilities-reits.md`
  - `etfs-and-unknown-stocks.md`

## Owner uploads
Drop documents here (PDFs, Markdown/text notes, annual reports, research write-ups,
articles, your own ideas) and commit them to `main`. The GitHub web UI's
"Add file → Upload files" works. The analyst reads new files on its next run and records
what it took from each below.
