## Process note
The ABNB decision was made on intraday price action rather than a confirmed close (this
run happens at 14:45 ET, market closes at 16:00 ET). I treated this as acceptable because
the technical level in question (the Nov 6 gap boundary, ~184) had already been pierced on
an intraday basis for the second session in a row, the stock was trading comfortably inside
the gap rather than right at the edge, and it's been four consecutive weak-to-down sessions
with no sign of buyers stepping in -- waiting for the literal close would mean waiting until
tomorrow's AM run regardless, by which point the position could have moved further against
the stop with no ability to act. Separately, this is useful confirmation that watching the
gap boundary itself (not just a round-number soft stop) is the more precise invalidation
signal for a PEAD trade -- worth keeping as the standard for future playbook A entries.
