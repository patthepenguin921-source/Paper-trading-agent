## Process note
Distinguishing "this stock has its own confirmed catalyst" (PANW's print) from "this stock is
rallying because something else happened" (CRWD, AMD, AVGO) was the main discipline exercised
today -- the latter group had real, verifiable news behind the moves, but verifiable news
about a *different* company or a macro event is not the same quality of signal as the name's
own numbers. Worth keeping as an explicit check alongside the existing "don't chase the first
green day" and "catalyst explains the move, not that entry timing is still good" rules.
